\section{Errata Noticed in the Paper}\label{app:errata}

While implementing the paper we noticed the following. Items 1 and 2 are corrected in our code; the
others did not affect the implementation. We do not claim that any of them invalidates the paper's proof.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0pt]
\item \textsf{KeyGen} of the repaired scheme prints $pk_i=s_i$; we implement $pk_i=s_iP$, as in Verma's
      scheme.
\item The repaired scheme's certificate check is printed with $R_{1i}$, a symbol from Verma's scheme; we
      check $c_iP=R_i+H_0(id_i\concat pk_i\concat R_i)\PTA$.
\item \textsf{Setup} declares $H_1$ and $H_2$ over inputs with no slot for $T_i$, while \textsf{Sign} and
      the verification hash six fields including $T_i$; we follow \textsf{Sign}.
\item In the proof of Lemma~3, the $H_1$ oracle table omits $T_i$, the domain of $H_0$ omits one group
      element, and the secret-key query returns $pk_i$.
\item $\mathbb{Z}_q^{*}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_p^{*}$ are used interchangeably.
\item The cost rows of Tables~II and~III for the repaired scheme equal Verma's rows and understate the
      scheme's own algorithm (\cref{sec:cost}); Fig.~4 inherits the error. The \textsf{AggSign} row
      overstates it. Verma's \textsf{Sign} row omits one point addition.
\item Table~II rates Verma's scheme as secure against $\Fone$, which the key-only forgery of
      \cref{sec:keyonly} contradicts.
\end{enumerate}
