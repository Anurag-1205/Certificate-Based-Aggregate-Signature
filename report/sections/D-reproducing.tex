\section{Reproducing the Results}\label{app:repro}

The implementation, tests and recorded measurements (\nolinkurl{bench/results/}) are in the project
repository; it needs Python~3.10+, libsodium~1.0.18+ and OpenSSL~3.0. From its root:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
Command & What it does \\
\midrule
\code{make selftest} & 62 algebra checks on both group libraries \\
\code{make test} & 424 tests (199 on each backend, 26 backend-independent) \\
\code{make attack} & the paper's attack, its failure on the repair, and the key-only forgery \\
\code{make nonce} & nonce-reuse key recovery and the hedged-nonce fix \\
\code{make bench} & timing sweep and cost analysis on both libraries (\cref{sec:cost}) \\
\code{make hashcost} & the hash-cost model for the P-256 residual (\cref{sec:p256-gap}) \\
\code{make optimize} & naive vs optimised verification, both schemes (\cref{sec:opt}) \\
\code{make dos} & aggregator policies under faults (\cref{sec:agg}) \\
\code{verify\_claims\_final.py} & recomputes and asserts every number in this report \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
